Шта се чува на уређају

Микрофон се користи само за вежбе изговора и само уз дозволу (може се одбити — апликација ради и без њега). Ако тај сервис није доступан, апликација снима кратак исечак на телефону, одмах га пушта и замењује следећим; исечак се никуда не шаље.

English

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

The app “Bukvar” (com.maxaxa.bukvar) is made by Maxaxa. Contact: hornsdron@gmail.com.

In short

Bukvar is for young children learning to read and for their parents. The app has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect, sell or share personal data of children or parents.

What is stored on the device

Learning progress (stars, friends, stickers), settings, the daily time limit, the child’s first name (only if a parent enters it, used only on a diploma image) and voice samples a parent records. All of this stays on the device and is removed with “reset progress” in the parent section or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on, the operating system may back up this data to the user’s Google account — this is done by Android, not by us.

Microphone

The microphone is optional and used only for pronunciation exercises, after permission is granted (it can be refused; the app works without it). When an internet connection is available, pronunciation is checked by the Android speech recognition service (usually Google), in which case audio may be processed by Google under its own privacy policy. If that service is not available, the app records a short clip on the device, plays it back immediately and overwrites it with the next one; the clip is never uploaded.

Purchases

The full version is bought by an adult through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; we do not see or store payment details. The app only remembers that the purchase was made. The Google Play Billing library may send technical reports to Google under Google’s policies.

Sharing

A parent can save or share a diploma or recordings through the Android share sheet — only by their own choice, in the parent section protected by a parental gate (press-and-hold plus an arithmetic question).

Children

The app is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
